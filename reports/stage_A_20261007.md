## What was built
- `src/dxftrain/data/extract_objects.py`: per-object, name-free extraction. Each DXF runs in its own subprocess with a timeout. It **replaces** `build_object_dataset.py`, which was not available anywhere on this machine.
- `data/build.py` (batch + skip log), `duplicate_report.py`, `make_dataset.py` (labels, project split, augmentation, immutable dataset), `datacard.py`, `labelling_sheet.py`. Configs live in `configs/data.yaml`, `augment.yaml` and `split.yaml`.

  - `contracts/categories.v1.json`: 54 categories plus `unknown`.
  - `prompts/classify_system.v1.txt`.
